# Flygande Jacob
Adapted from: https://www.ica.se/recept/flygande-jacob-717569/
## Ingredients
- 550 g chicken
- 280 g bacon
- 1x banana
- 3 dl cultured cream
- 1 dl chili sauce
- 2 tsk sambal oelek
- 1 dl peanuts
- salt
- black pepper
## Procedure
1. Cut the banana into pieces
2. Cut the chicken into pieces
3. Chop the bacon
4. Fry the chicken until no raw parts are visible
5. Season the chicken with salt and pepper
6. Transfer the chicken to an ovenproof dish
7. Fry the bacon
8. Add the bacon on top of the chicken
9. Add the banana pieces on top of the chicken
10. Add cultured cream, chili sauce, and sambal oelek to a bowl
11. Mix the content of the bowl and then pour the mixture on top of the chicken
12. Bake in the oven at 225°C for approximately 20 minutes
