# Eggplant Parmigiana
Adapted from: https://www.recipetineats.com/eggplant-parmigiana
## Ingredients
- 5x aubergines
- 1x yellow onion
- 5 garlic cloves
- 400 g crushed tomatoes
- 500 g tomato passata
- 1 msk fresh oregano (about 3 branches)
- 150 g parmesan
- 150 g mozzarella
- 1 pot of basil
- 1 krm sugar
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Olive oil
## Procedure
1. Cut the aubergines in 8 mm thick slices
2. Brush each side of each slice with olive oil and put on oven trays with baking paper
3. Bake in a fan-assisted oven at 200 °C for about 20 minutes, or until lightly browned
4. Chop the onion
5. Finely chop the garlic
6. Wash the oregano, pull off the leaves from the branches, and chop it
7. Fry yellow onion and garlic until transparent in oliv oil
8. Add tomato passata, crushed tomato, oregano, salt, and pepper
9. Gently boil for 30 minutes until it is reduced to a thick sauce
10. Grate the parmesan into a separate plate
11. Smear some tomato sauce on the base of an oven safe dish
12. Spread a third of the aubergine on the bottom of the dish
13. Spread a third of the tomato sauce on top of the aubergine
14. Drizzle a little olive oil over the tomato sauce layer
15. Spread a third of the basil leaves in the dish
16. Spread a third of the grated parmesan over the basil leave layer
17. Repeat the layering from step 12 to 16 twice more, but save the last third of basil leaves on the side
18. Add grated mozzarella on top
19. Bake for 25 min in 180 °C, or until the mozzarella is golden
