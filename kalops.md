# Kalops
Adapted from: https://www.koket.se/jens_linder/soppor_och_grytor/kott/skansk_kalops
## Ingredients
- 1.2 kg chuck steak
- 4x yellow onions
- 3 msk butter
- 6x carrots
- 5 msk flour
- 3x bay leaves
- 18x whole black pepper corns
- White pepper
- 1 msk salt
- Water
## Procedure
1. Cut the beef into large cubes (about 3x3 cm)
2. Cut the onion into wedges
3. Pre-fry the beef in butter and season it with salt and pepper
4. Move the fried beef into a pot
5. Fry the onion wedges in butter until transparent, then add them to the same pot as the beef
6. Add some water to the pan and heat it up, then add the water to the pot
7. Add the bay leaves to the pot
8. Add the pepper corns to the pot
9. Add approximately 6 dl of water to the pot
10. Let the content of the pot gently boil for 30 min
11. Cut the carrots and add to the pot
12. Continue gently boiling for 60 minutes
13. Combine the flour and some water, then add to the pot
14. Boil for 5 min
## Serving Suggestions
Mashed potatoes: Boil 1 kg potatoes, mash, add 2.25 dl milk, 50 g butter, 0.75 tsk salt, 2 krm white pepper, 1 krm sugar
