# Salmon gratin
Adapted from: https://www.jennysmatblogg.nu/2016/01/02/laxgratang/
## Ingredients
- 4x salmon fillets
- 2 dl creme fraiche
- 2.5 dl cream
- 1x lemon (grated lemon zest)
- 2 msk lemon juice
- 20 g dill
- 1 msk lobster stock
- 1 krm salt
- 120 g Västerbottensost (aged for 24 months)
- 140 g shrimps
## Procedure
1. Defrost the salmon fillets if needed
2. Wash and chop the dill
3. Set aside a little dill for topping, and add the rest to a bowl
4. Grate the lemon zest into the bowl
5. Add the creme fraiche, cream, lemon juice, lobster stock, and salt to the bowl
6. Mix the contents of the bowl until well combined and smooth
7. Place the defrosted salmon fillets in an ovenproof dish
8. Pour the sauce over the salmon and top with the grated cheese
9. Bake in the oven at 220°C for approximately 25 minutes, or until golden brown
10. Top with the shrimp and dill before serving
